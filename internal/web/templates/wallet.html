Wallet

Allocation moves no sats

The wallet balance is a spending authorisation, not a pot of segregated sats. Allocating 100,000 sats moves nothing on Lightning: no invoice is paid, no channel changes, and the sats stay exactly where they were. What changes is that BrollyZapper will now authorise up to 100,000 sats of outbound payments and refuse the next one. Deallocating is the same in reverse.

Payments only you can settle

This app asked your node about these payments and could not find out what happened to them. Each one is still holding part of your spending ceiling, and it will keep holding it until you say what became of it.

Check at your node first. Look up the payment hash in your node's own records — lncli listpayments, or your node's interface — and see whether it settled. This app is asking you to state a fact it could not establish, and what you say here becomes the ledger.
